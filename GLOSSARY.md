# Jungle Tribe

Jungle Tribe connects people who collect plants with sitters who provide in-home plant care. Plant collections can be used independently of the care marketplace.

## Language

**Collector**:
A person who documents plants in one or more collections, whether or not they book care.

**Customer**:
A person who requests or books plant care from a sitter. A customer does not need to document a plant collection to book care.
_Avoid_: Buyer, client

**Sitter**:
An individual who offers in-home plant care through Jungle Tribe. Active sitters have verified identity and a public documented plant collection; identity verification does not certify plant-care expertise.
_Avoid_: Vendor, seller

**Collection**:
A named grouping of a collector's documented plants, such as the plants in a living room or greenhouse. A collection may be private or publicly shared.
_Avoid_: Inventory

**Plant**:
An individual plant documented in a collection, with its own details and photo history.

**Booking**:
An arrangement for one selected sitter to provide care over one or more visits, grouped into a single checkout.
_Avoid_: Order, reservation

**Visit**:
One occurrence of in-home care within a booking, with a requested date, duration, time window, and services.
_Avoid_: Booking when referring to only one occurrence in a multi-visit booking

**Service**:
A kind of plant care offered by a sitter and requested for a visit, such as routine watering, repotting, or pest treatment.

**Specialty**:
A sitter's stated area of plant-care experience, such as orchids or anthuriums. A specialty is a quality signal, not a certification.

**Platform dispute**:
A disagreement about a booking, service failure, payment, or refund that Jungle Tribe handles under its platform rules.
_Avoid_: Insurance claim

**Insurance claim**:
A request for coverage under a third-party insurance policy, evaluated by the insurer. It is distinct from a platform dispute and exists only when applicable coverage is offered.
_Avoid_: Platform guarantee
